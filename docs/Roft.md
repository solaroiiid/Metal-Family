## Biography

## Appearance

Roft is quite tall, thin build. His jaw and cheekbones are quite sharp. Eyebrow arched. His hair is gray and long, tied into a low ponytail with a black elastic band. He wears a classic suit with a gray shirt, black jacket, black pants, and classic black shoes. He always tries to keep his posture

and with everyone except the owner of the estate, he speaks in a contemptuous tone.

## Personality

Roft has a rather arrogant character. He does not sympathize with Sebastian, but on the contrary, he helps Gustav find his "wrongdoings". For example, he tells Gustav that Sebastian knows Ches.

## Relationships

Glam (as Sebastian)

Sebastian and Roft have a rather tense relationship. Sebastian tries to behave respectfully towards him, which Roft himself does not show. Roft, on the orders of Gustav, checks Sebastian's room, reproaches the young violinist and tries in every possible way to show his disdain for him.
