## Gallery

Victoria, also known as Vicky, is the wife of Glam and the mother of Dee and Heavy.

## Personality

Vicky is a very strong and short tempered woman and doesn't have the best social awareness. She isn't afraid to fight at the drop of a hat. More so if someone hurts her or her family. She is best described as being hypermasculine or tomboyish, as she is interested in motorcycles and doesn’t appear to like “girly” things. She is very vulgar and is prone to excessive swearing and general aggressive behavior.

## Appearance

Vicky is tall and muscular, just like the rest of the members of her biker gang. She has has waist- length orange-red hair and green eyes. She is usually seen wearing a leather vest and leather boots with black pants and a black t-shirt. She typically wears her long hair in a braid down her back with two wavy strands on each side of her head. She also has a black tribal-tattoo on her right upper arm.
