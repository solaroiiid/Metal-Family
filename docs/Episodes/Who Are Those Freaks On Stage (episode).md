## Summary

The episode starts with Glam writing down on his new diary about his life with the band while Chive and Bob argue about directions on their way to their next stop. As years go by, their band becomes more and more successful until one day after performing "Smells Like Cherry", their manager announces that their band has been chosen to play for none other than Twisted Sister, much to the guys' delight. While Ches takes drugs nonstop, the manager then advises the band to start singing love songs that are "more lyric" despite Ches' protests that "Smells Like Cherry" is about food but gives in when Bob and Lordi vote in favor of the love songs. After Ches leaves, the manager tells Glam to talk to Ches to get him to stop taking drugs, to which Glam agrees.

At their hotel room, Ches asks Glam to send a letter for him with money for Ches' mother. Glam agrees but also asks Ches to stop doing drugs, to which Ches agrees to but the next day at the reheasal Ches shows up high with a song that doesn't even make sense so Bob and Lordy leave with along with a disappointed Glam. That night, Glam writes on his diary about Ches seemingly ruining their chance to play with Twisted Sister when a remorseful Ches shows up and gives Glam some earrings he wanted and apologizes for his behavior. Glam accepts his apology but on the condition that Ches gets rid of all his drugs, which Ches does albeit he ends up clogging the toilet when Ches throws them there so they spend the night cleaning up and piercing Glam's ears, with their conversation making Bob and Lordy mistakenly believe that the two were having sex.

At the last reheasal, Ches arrives sober with the new song and everything is going well until Liza arrives and tells Chive that a woman was asking for him and that she used his real name, which prompts Chive to leave the reheasal and answer the phone, believing the caller to be his mother, but instead is his aunt Rita, who reveals to Ches that his mother was found dead in her house. This shocks and deeply saddens Ches to the point were he forgets about the reheasal and gets drunk. Thirty minutes later, the band is still waiting so Glam decides to go after Chive and nds him in an alleyway drunk. A frustrated Glam starts ranting about how he tried to help Chive but that it was all in vain so he decides to walk out on Chive until the latter reveals that his mother
