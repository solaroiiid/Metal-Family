Protector is the second episode of 2018 russian web series "Metal Family".

## Plot

Dee tries to defend Heavy from a bully.

## Summary

Dee was helping Curlton cheat on an exam when he saw Gopher and Heavy getting into a ght and insulting each other's music until Gopher beat up Heavy to the point of leaving him motionless on the ground and Dee, wanting to protect his younger brother, tried to get Gopher to stop by claiming that Heavy has had enough and trying to oer the bully to help him cheat on his exams so that Gopher can get the game he wants but Dee makes a wrong choice of words that lead Gopher to believe Dee was calling him dumb and this resulted on Gopher punching Dee so hard that he had diculty helping Curlton with his exam (it was an oral exam and Dee was so hurt that it was hard for Curlton to understand Dee through the microphone).
