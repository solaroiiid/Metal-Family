Do You Play The Guitar? is the seventh episode of Season 1.

## Plot

Dee tries to learn how to play the guitar, with unexpected results.

## Summary

The episode begins with Glam making dinner when Dee walks in holding a guitar and ask his father if he can use it, to which Glam says yes. In Dee's room, Dee is watching a tutorial that teaches how to play a guitar but Dee quickly grows frustrated since he cannot get a single note right. Heavy then walks in without knocking and Dee chews him out for that, Heavy then notices the guitar that Dee just dropped on the oor and asks if he can play it himself, to which Dee says yes and is shocked to see that Heavy is perfectly capable of playing the instrument without much eort. Dee then resorts to asks his father to teach him how to play the guitar but Glam rudely states that his eldest son has no musical talent and that he will never be a rock star or anything of the sort but Dee cuts him o and insists until Glam agrees.

The lesson doesn't go well since Dee is too impatient and Glam oers little to no help but when Dee wonders out loud how Heavy is capable of playing the guitar while Dee cannot Glam goes on a tirade about how Heavy has muscular memory and has natural talent while Dee does not and his speech quickly escalates to yelling at Dee for making a fool of his family and himself

until Dee sadly walks away, Glam regains his composure and, with his smile back on his face, admits he was too rough and asks his son to not be mad at him. Dee lies and says he is not before walking away with the guitar he had borrowed earlier.

Dee is last seen back in his room, where he opens his laptop and it is revealed that he was trying to play the guitar to impress a girl when she asked him "Do you play the guitar?". Dee simply writes "Nope" and closes his laptop.
