Quest is the sixth episode of "Metal Family".

## Plot

The family participates on a game of "Escape Room", with each family member going out their way to win.

## Summary

The episode starts with the Metal Family arriving to an "Escape Room" for a family game. As they walk, an excited Heavy tells the family about how the game is so scary it made Curlton soil himself and wonders what room it was while Dee asks why would he want to see the room "were [Curlton's] shit was laid". Victoria then reprimands her sons for saying swear words in public but Dee denies he did, prompting an angry Vicky to literally smack him and Heavy so hard she sends them ying into the waiting room were the secretary awaits for them, which makes Vicky slightly embarrassed and Glam explains that it was a "learning process". The secretary then continues by explaining the rules of the game and telling the family that since they are four they can split into "parents vs children" or "boys vs girls" teams, mistaking Heavy for a girl, much to Dee's amusement and his brother's and parents' annoyance. The embarrassed secretary tries to shrug it o and continues before leaving the room.

A mustached man disguised as a monster leads the family into two separate rooms and locks

the parents and children teams into two their quest rooms and the game begins. Dee and Heavy start a make believe game about the ones in charge of the quests secretly eating people. Meanwhile, Vicky tries to resolve the riddle unaware that Glam is solving it while she is talking. A skeleton appears behind Vicky and she punches it out of instinct while Glam takes it's one eyeball. Vicky comes to the conclusion that she has to rip some brick o the wall and refuses Glam's help. Eventually Vicky steps on a certain spot, a hatch opens and Glam is able to solve the rst riddle and open a door using the eyeball and a key. Vicky steps into the room but it shuts down behind her, at the same time the wall opens and Dee appears, also having been separated from his brother and so the teams switch to Dee and Glam vs Heavy and Vicky. Dee and Glam are able to solve the riddles easily while Vicky encourages Heavy to instead use force and rips a hammer o the wall to rip a hole through the wall and escape despite the mustached man's warnings, who resorts to go and stop Vicky himself after the quest is over but Vicky refuses to listen, believing that it's a part of the game, and has Heavy use the hammer to make a hole through the wall while she holds the door. Once Heavy is done, Vicky grabs him and jumps

through the hole.

Once mother and son are on the street oor they nd Glam and Dee waiting for them. Vicky asks how they got out so quickly and Dee attempts to explain but is interrupted by Glam, who subtly tells him to play along so they let Vicky and Heavy believe they won and Vicky gloats nonstop as the family walks away.
