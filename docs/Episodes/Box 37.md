Box 37 is the fth episode of "Metal Family".

## Plot

Glam's father dies and leaves his estranged son a box labelled "BOX 37".

## Summary

The episode starts with Vicky reluctantly putting on a black dress per Glam's request to go to the

probate of Glam's father to receive inheritance after his passing even though Glam is not concerned about it. Once Vicky is done Glam compliments her, making her blush, and she tells him that he better puts on something just as ridiculous as her dress as he leaves the room.

As Vicky heads downstairs she spots someone who looks like Glam in a dress and Vicky laughs at "his" silly outt before the real Glam shows up behind the woman who he introduces as his sister, Lydia, much to Vicky's shock since they both look identical. Lydia dismisses her sister-in- law and tells them to get going.

In Lydia's limousine, Vicky complains how uncomfortable she is traveling in the backseat and is not allowed to sit in the front, then she asks why do her and Glam have to go and Glam tells her that all heads of the family have to attend, with Lydia chuckling at the fact that Glam referred to himself as "family".

At the probate, almost everything is given to Lydia, who gives her brother a smug expression even though he doesn't mind at all while Victoria is so horried over the fact that all the valuables go to her and Glam's cousin Vallen and therefore she and her family are not getting anything that she collapses into the oor. When everyone thinks the reading is over Lydia asks for her father's yacht but it appears to go missing so Lydia rants, further angering Vicky since Lydia now has "enough to aord the Titanic" while the rest prepare to leave until the lawyer resumes reading the will and announces that Glam has been left an old cardboard box only referred to as "Box 37" and both Lydia and Vicky think that the key to a yacht is inside, an eager Vicky tells Glam to open it quickly, Lydia threatens to sue Glam if the yacht keys are there and Glam just looks down at the box in shock, apparently not hearing anything that goes on around him.

Glam and Vicky take a cab to go home, this time with Vicky getting her wish to travel in the front seat while ranting about Lydia to the driver and tells Glam to open the box. Glam, with the box on his lap and the smile back on his face, states that he isn’t interested in it's contents, but Vicky says that she wants to know and so he opens it. He doesn’t say what is in it, but enters an almost dissociative state once he sees it, his smile once again gone as he stares out the window and the episode fades to black without a real answer as to what it is.
