## Plot

Glam and Ches decide to part ways after an argument.

## Summary

The episode starts with Ches' mother in her home, were she is sobbing and apologizing to Ches for being a horrible mother before dropping a damaged spoon and a bottle of alcohol on the envelope that Ches had sent her not too long ago.

At the motel, Ches is screaming at Glam since the latter decided not to go to the audition they had arranged with their possible new manager while Glam calmly explains that he met a girl named Victoria while holding up her keys but Ches is outraged and yells at Glam for skipping their rst audition and now the second one for a "c**t with tits", earning a slap on the face from Glam. Ches then leaves but has trouble getting the Pantera to work, Glam takes the chance to apologize but claims he can't leave until he nds Victoria but Ches is not fazed by this until Glam asks if Ches really wants to leave. Ches says no but states that he had always dreamed of recognition and drives away and so Glam resumes his search for Victoria.

As Ches drives away he listens to the radio and hears about how his former bandmates, Lordi and Bob, are doing great since he and Glam left, with Lordi becoming the band's leader and their new vocalist, Leo, who made changes to the song such as singing it with high notes and claiming that it is about a girl who smells like cherry, making Ches angrier to the point of throwing his van's radio out the window. Ches' audition is a success and he forms a new band called "Green Sperm Whale" with musicians that resemble Glam, Lordi and Bob. At the party after a concert, Ches nds himself unable to use drugs but after a short talk with a girl he does and passes out.

Ches then nds himself back in his teens surrounded by clouds and sees his mother coming for him. Ches apologizes for being a "bad" son while she tells him everything is ne but then Ches abruptly wakes up in a hospital, apparently having had an overdose and whispers "come back"....
