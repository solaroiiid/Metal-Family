Stasik Contra is the third episode of Season 1.

## Plot

An anti-smoking group goes around the community trying to get the locals to quit their bad habit.

## Summary

An anti-smoking group goes around the city lecturing people about not smoking in public spaces before spraying their cigarettes with water, much to their chagrin. The group then nds Victoria, who is smoking with Dee. As they were about to leave a fast food restaurant, the anti- smoking group proceed to lecture her as well until she gets fed up with them and beats them up. While his mother is busy, Dee picks up the camera that they had dropped.

Later, Victoria and Dee are sitting at the police station while the anti-smokers tell the police what she did to them and how polite her son was to give them back the cameras. Victoria tries to play victim until she realizes that their cameras recorded and cringes until the disgusted ocers nds some kind of porn involving cats on the video instead of the real footage, revealing that Dee had swapped the videos in order to save his mother from being arrested before returning the cameras and so without evidence, Victoria is allowed to leave with no consequences.

After returning home, Dee is seen in his room showing Heavy the actual footage of their mother beating up the anti smokers, much to the boys' amusement. Heavy asks if their wounds can be stitched but Dee, chuckling, states that he doesn't think they can.
