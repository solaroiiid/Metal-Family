The episode begins with Heavy peeking into his father's oce and tries to get his father's attention but Glam tells him that he is busy until Heavy tells him that he was summoned in the school. Glam than asks what is the reason for that.

In the ashback, Ursula complained about Heavy's appearance and behavior, she even complained about how Heavy didn't wash his hair and declared that since her constant phone calls to Heavy's parents were always ignored she went so far as to have two other teachers hold him still while Ursula cut a chunk of Heavy's hair, leaving him with a bald patch in order to "send

a message" and get his parents to come over at once even though she had no right to do so.

In the present Glam, noticeably angered at what they had done to his son, leaves everything he is doing, turns on the lights and, with his smile back on he agrees to go to Heavy's school before getting himself ready for the meeting.

At the school, Ursula, Mildred and Drusilla wait for Heavy's parents to show up as they talk about how Heavy must come from a bad family since normal parents couldn't name their son "Heavy" and Ursula claims that she wouldn't be surprised if the boy's father doesn't show up.
