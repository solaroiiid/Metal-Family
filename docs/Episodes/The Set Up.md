All of this happened recently. Now, facing threats from a dealer after losing an important package, Bug had to confront this man. The person who might know more about this... was perhaps Glam himself, who, well... had become very close to Victoria since the accident. A very big problem.

Then, we see what happened over several months at the clinic. Glam had achieved his goal, he was close to Vicky, both with broken bones, but together! Eventually, we see how they formed a friendship, after their recovery, they both enjoyed moments together and particularly "cute" ones, and nally, dating.

Glam knew exactly how to push Bug's buttons; the deal was done. Meanwhile, Victoria gets into trouble at a women's lingerie store, where she ends up stealing a specic item. The police were fed up with it all: robberies, murders, gangs... There's never any rest for them argument.

The episode culminates with all the main characters from this past storyline together, a very wonderful and... unexpected ending?
