## Appearance

Dee strongly takes after his father, having his blonde hair (although in slightly brighter shade, likely influenced from his mother's brighter tone of hair), his blue eyes, his rectangle jaw and his slim silhouette, but he also has his mother's curly hair, freckles, and round nose.

His hair is mid-length and tied back into a low ponytail with a single strand falling into his face. He is usually dressed in a black T-shirt under an unbuttoned white jacket with rolled up sleeves, grayish-blue pants and black boots. He also sports several accessories, like black wristbands and bracelets, dark eyeliner, a black choker and two helix piercings on each ear. He is almost always seen with a tired/gloomy expression on his face. He also speaks in a deep nasally-voice.

## Personality

Much like with his appearance, he strongly takes after his father, having his quiet disposition, high intelligence, and sadistic tendencies, but he also has his mother's unfriendly attitude and bad temper.

In "Protector", we see Dee helping other students cheat on their exams in exchange for money and defending his younger brother, Heavy, from Chipmunk. This shows us how he uses his intellect to make money & benefit himself, and helps to establish his role as a (albeit, non-admittedly) caring older brother. These traits are strengthened further in the show, where we see him using his skills with technology to clear cameras of evidence that would've incriminated his mother and add fake evidence to frame the guys pressing charges for illegal videos on said cameras. Dee also has access to the school's security cameras as shown in the episode "Lif". In this episode it is also implied he has a history of blackmailing students and making many deals, though, he does always keep his word.

Dee is also a very good liar. We see in the episode "Poker" the only person able to see through his façade is his father after grilling him, and, during his Q&A he lies about the reason he sneaks out at night (though why he sneaks out isn't confirmed, we know he isn't "just going to the bathroom" because he is wearing a backpack as he sneaks out in one of Heavy's instagram posts). He lies about not watching My Little Pony, and he lies very easily to his father about the rat he has hiding in his room.

Dee also knows five languages, and has been shown doing multiple subjects of homework at once in sketches, and is very intuitive with most people's moves. We see this in "Stasik Contra" each time he ducks before his mom swings her hands as she talks, and in the poker episode when he ducks before Victoria spits out her drink. He likes to collect information on people. He knows Lif's schedule, habits, and where she lives, and it's also implied he has stalked Gopher a bit since he knew that he would stare at the PlayStation on display in a store window after school.

It is also noted that Dee might potentially be a pyromaniac. This is backed with many official sketches being drawn of him setting things on fire, and only ever smiling during these times. He is also smiling while setting something on fire with Heavy in one of his Instagram posts. Despite being good at many things, Dee is shown to struggle with picking up playing the guitar while Heavy is able to play almost right away. This frustrates Dee, and after an argument with his father, he decides to give up on trying to learn.
