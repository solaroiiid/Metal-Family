Ursula is Heavy's teacher. She is very mean to Heavy to the point of bullying him due to her not liking his unconventional lifestyle.

## Biography

Ursula complained about Heavy's appearance and behavior, such as Heavy not washing his hair and declared that since her constant phone calls to Heavy's parents were always ignored so she took drastic measures and went so far as to have two other teachers hold him still while Ursula cut a chunk of Heavy's hair, leaving him with a bald patch in order to "send a message" and get his parents to come over at once even though she had no right to do so.

At the school, Ursula, Mildred and Drusilla wait for Heavy's parents to show up as they talk about how Heavy must come from a bad family since normal parents couldn't name their son "Heavy" and Ursula claims that she wouldn't be surprised if the boy's father doesn't show up. As on cue, Glam arrives and his appearance shocks Ursula so much that she spits her coee into the teacher next to him.

Glam greets the teachers and sits down, he asks to be called Glam instead of Sebastian but Ursula shrugs him o and tells him that his son's looks and manners are unacceptable in their school yet Glam, with his calm face slowly turning into a frown, asks if it is acceptable to take drastic measures (i.e. cutting Heavy's hair). Ursula is at first taken aback by his response but she quickly answers that it is considering his son's behavior, appearance and "lack of education". Glam then tells her that if this is the case then he considers "this" normal before he leaves. The teachers are briey shocked until one of them points out a small pink bomb sitting in front of

their desk and before they can react the bomb goes o and the teachers, along with the entire classroom, are covered in pink glitter and stars.

A week later, the classroom still has some glitter left as Ursula is berating Heavy again as she is still not happy that he keeps showing "inappropriate" behavior and demands to see his mother urgently since she doesn't want to be around Glam again and Ursula believes that she will be able to reason with Heavy's mother since they are both women. Heavy says that she will be angry about this, which pleases Ursula, thinking that he means his mother will be mad at him but Heavy tries to explain that it's best not to make his mother angry just as Victoria shows up at the school's entrance with a large hammer.

## Appearance

Ursula is an overweight woman with red hair in an updo. She wears a pink dress, a pearl necklace and red lipstick.

## Personality

Ursula has no qualms on yelling at and insulting Heavy since the boy's appareance and behavior are not of her liking and feels entitled to bully her student by cutting a large amount of his hair to get his parents to come to the school so she can convince them to punish him and is very happy when she thinks Heavy's mother will be mad at him.
