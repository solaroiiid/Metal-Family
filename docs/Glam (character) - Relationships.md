Mary Shvagenbagen

Mary is Glam's mother. She a submissive woman who strongly disapproved of Gustav's abuse of Glam but was too weak to stand up for her son and she also treated him with some severity. It is unknown how Mary felt after Glam ran away.

Lydia Shvagenbagen

Lydia is Glam's older sister. In her debut, she is shown insulting and dismissive of Glam, even resenting him. She had even threatened legal action against him for the yacht, showing she prefers materialism over her brother. She is bitter towards him.

In the Glam's past ashback arc, she liked tattling on him and would always tell on him, never keeping a secret for him, unless she was bribed to do so. She would often seem him as inferior and would condescend him, most likely due to some sort of inuence or pressure from their father.

However, it is revealed that her reason for resenting him and being bitter is because Glam had abandoned her and the family after Gustav, their father, had found out she had kept Glam's secret. This lead to him likely beating the truth out of her, and had destroyed his secret Box 37 project, which ultimately lead to Glam deciding to disassociate with Gustav and the rest of the family. Lydia, despite her up-tight and cruel nature, was terried that Glam left, and whispered

for him desperately, quietly and indirectly begging for him to not go.

Despite all she's done against her brother during their childhood, it was likely out of her own survival in an unhealthy, abusive household, rather than genuine antagonization. Unfortunately, her father's abuse had carried on to aect her in her adulthood, shown when she is seen insulting Glam and seemingly very unhappy to see him years later.

Victoria

Vicky is Glam's wife. Glam fell in love with Vicky at rst sight and even gave up his musician career to get her but Vicky simply dismissed him as an idiot and ignored him but Glam refused to give up and went so far as to let Vicky run him over with her bike to get her attention and return her missing keys to her. This made Vicky give him a chance and they got to know each other and eventually they got married and had two sons; Dee and Heavy.

Vicky will defend Glam ercely against any of her fellow bikers who make fun of him and even get into ghts for him as mentioned in Glam's Q&A.

Dee

Dee is Glam's rstborn son. Dee seems to look up to him as he dresses and tries to act just like his father (albeit in lacking any musical talent). Glam loves his son very much, yet he has no qualms in telling Dee he will never be able to play music. Glam apologized, but in his Q&A, Glam defended himself by claiming he was just being straightforward, not mean.

Heavy

Heavy is Glam's youngest son. Glam loves his son very much, and Heavy has stated that his father is great, albeit Heavy seems to be closer to Vicky. Glam also seems impressed by Heavy's natural talent for music.

## Other

Anna

Anna is Vicky's friend. Glam has yet to interact with Anna on screen yet it seems like Anna isn't fond of Glam since she seemed really annoyed in Vicky's Q&A when she got asked many times about Glam. Anna also has no qualms in calling Glam "blondie" (a very rude insult in Russia), albeit she did so to get Vicky so angry she could win an arm wrestling match against Bug.

Lif

Lif is Dee's classmate and crush. Glam seems to approve of Lif despite her having apophenia. It is implied that Glam is aware of Dee's crush on her as he attempted to talk to Dee about her before Vicky interrupted him.
