## Family

## Parents

Nothing is known about Vicky's relationship with her parents since she described their relationship as both normal and non-existent.

## Glam

Victoria and Glam are very dierent in character, but this does not prevent them from being sincerely happy next to each other, because they both accept each other for who they are. Victoria always supports her husband in dicult moments for him and cannot stand it when someone tries to belittle him, for example, when Anna called Glam a "blondie", Victoria got very angry and was ready to start a fight for such words in relation to her husband. She respects Glam, can sometimes be a little rude to him, but is unlikely to ever really want to oend him.

## Dee

Dee and Vicky

Dee is the Victoria's eldest son. The two had little one on one interaction but they seem to get along well such as when she took Dee out to a fast food restaurant and he saved her from being arrested for beating up some anti-smockers for annoying her. However, Vicky is often annoyed by Dee getting the upper hand on a discussion to the point were she wanted to check his phone just to spite him and calling him a "jerk". Victoria and Dee have a good relationship, but still less warm than with Heavy. Since Dee is a secretive, silent guy, he rarely tells something to his mother, especially since they are very dierent in character, because he went more to his father than to his mother. And all the same, Victoria loves her son, just like he loves her, she listens and takes Dee quite seriously, since after his words in one of the episodes, where she was asked to

put out a cigarette on the street, Vicky stopped for a short while, but still.

## Heavy

Vicky and Heavy

Victoria is the mother of Heavy. The two get along well due to having many things in common from appearance to personality. In his Q&A, Heavy said he prefers to spend time with his mother, even if it hurts Heavy when she tickles him and she enjoys taunting Heavy. Victoria has a noticeably warmer relationship with Heavy than with Dee. This is most likely because Heavy is a much more open and mother-like child, which makes it easier to get along with him. Victoria loves her son very much, she can sometimes yell at him or scold him, but she always remains on his side in any case. As a mother, not the most responsible, but she speaks with her son very honestly, sincerely, understandingly.

## Friends

## Anna

Anna and Vicky have been best friends before Vicky married Glam ever since Vicky stopped on the road to help Anna when the latter's bike had a problem. Anna enjoys spending time with Vicky albeit she doesn't seem to be fond of Vicky's family.

## Bug

Much has not been said about the relationship between Bug and Victoria, but it is known that they have known each other for a very long time and went to the gym together. Around the time Vicky met Glam, Bug and her seemed to have something going on between them or were at least friends but he wanted more since he couldn't let another man (Glam) near her and he attempted to kiss her but she refused and he was so angered when she squeezed his face roughly that he slapped her in the face, infuriating her enough to knock him out.

However, despite the violence the two decided to remain friends. Victoria trusts Bug and is not averse to doing something loud with him for fun, in one episode they arrange a fake competition together, at the end of which they share the money.

Enemies Lydia Shvagenbagen

Lydia is Glam's sister and therefore Victoria's sister-in-law. Lydia did not care in the slightest about Victoria or anything she had to say and later made Vicky and Glam take a cab home since she refused to drive them back in her limousine after throwing a tantrum over not getting a yacht, much to Victoria's chagrin since Lydia inherited from his father "enough to aord the Titanic".
