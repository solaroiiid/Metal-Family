Curlton is a young teenage boy who attends the same school as Dee and Heavy.

## History

### "Protector"

Curlton asks Dee for help to pass his oral exam that would take place after recess. As a solution, Dee gives him a hidden microphone to put on his ear so that he give him the answers.

After seeing Heavy provoking a bully called Chipmunk, he called him an idiot. Dee tried to defend Heavy, and got himself beat up by Chipmunk. Because of his injuries, Dee's cheeks swelled which gave him issues speaking and helping Curlton answer the exam.

## Appearance

Curlton is a young boy with curly brown hair, green/grey eyes and fair skin. He wears a burgundy sweater and a blue backpack.
