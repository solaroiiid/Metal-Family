Angelica was Heavy's classmates for many years and he used to pull on her pigtails, slip cockroaches down her collar and hide her backpack before class.

Despite everything, she developed a crush on Heavy and she secretly sent him a Valentine's Day card written in Japanese with the message "You are rude and stupid. I like you." since she can be seen looking at him through her hand mirror as he reads the card.

Angelica does not appear in "Apophenia" but Heavy mentions her as she was invited to his birthday party but later sent Heavy a text explaining that her parents won't let her go to Heavy's party.

**Appearance**
Angelica has slim figure, long blonde hair and aquamarine eyes. Since she is in Heavy's class, it can be assumed that she is around 13-15 years old. She seems to be very feminine in both her looks and mannerisms.

**Personality**
Angelica is a well-mannered, diligent girl who is also a good student.

**Trivia**
- She has yet to make an appearance in the show albeit she was seen in some of Heavy's Instagram posts. She was mentioned in "Present" but it was not confirmed that her name was Angelica until her name was revealed by Phinlana in Heavy's Instagram posts.
- Before her real name was revealed she was called "Card Girl" or "Blonde Girl" due to the Valentine's Day card she gave Heavy, similar to how Lif was often referred to by the fans as "Quest Girl" before her name was revealed in in the episode of the same name.
- In Heavy's Instagram it is revealed that Angelica knows some Japanese as she wrote Heavy's card in japanese.
- Alina Kovaleva's friend, Phinlana, gave away that Heavy's secret admirer was Angelica when she jokingly asked "Heavy" who the blonde girl was and Angelica was the only girl with blonde hair in the picture.
- Like Heavy, Angelica seems to like cats since she was seen with a cat in her lap in one of Heavy's selfies.
- Unlike the rest of the cast, she has a different outfit everytime she appears in Heavy's instagram.
- She is currently learning korean and japanese.
