Christopher — (Russian: Кристофер) — is a police officer from the 7th episode of the second season of the web series "Metal Family". He is a well-built man who wears shiny glasses; he has brown hair, and in everyday life he wears a yellow shirt and brown trousers. He is a friend of Glam, whom he met while drawing up a report on the collision with Victoria during a motorcycle trip in the mountains. He got along with him quite well and visited Glam in the hospital, where the two found common ground discussing scary stories. Later, Christopher helped Glam and cleared the file of Bug, Victoria's ex-boyfriend, thereby saving him from imprisonment, unlike Kit.

There is a theory that Wilhelm is Christopher's son, as indicated by the same build and the same shiny glasses.
