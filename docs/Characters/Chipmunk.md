### Relationships

- **Enemies:** Heavy; Dee

- **Dislikes:** Heavy metal music

### Status

- **Status:** Alive

Chipmunk is a bully who attends the same school as Dee and Heavy.

## History

### "Protector"

A fight broke out between Chipmunk and Heavy while Dee was helping Curlton cheat on an exam. The exchange between the two began after Heavy presumably insulted rap music, claiming that heavy metal was better, which ended with Chipmunk beating Heavy until he was motionless on the ground. In an attempt to protect his younger brother, Dee tried to get Chipmunk to stop, but he only wound up getting himself beat up, too. Having suffered injuries, Dee's cheeks swelled, so he had difficulty speaking to Curlton and helping him with his exam.

### "His Secrets"

During one of their chats, Lif asked Dee how his tooth was after Chipmunk broke it, and Dee replied that it was fine before asking if she liked rats.

## Appearance

He is a tall, heavyset teenager with buck teeth. He wears a white hoodie, a red beanie and golden jewelry.

## Personality

Chipmunk is a typical bully who likes to beat and pick on those smaller and weaker than him.

## Trivia

- He lives across the street from a video game store and hopes to get a new gaming console for his birthday. He likes rap music
- Heavy stated in his Q&A that he intends to become stronger so that he can beat up Chipmunk.
  - In the Q&A, Heavy also stated that Chipmunk hasn't been going to school anymore but he doesn't know why.

- Alina Kovaleva originally planned to make a comic were Dee and Heavy got revenge on him for bullying them but the idea was scrapped as she felt that they "had matured since then".
- In Heavy's Instagram, it's revealed that Chipmunk broke Dee's tooth before the events of "Lif".
