## Biography

Diana, along with the rest of Dee's classmates, were fascinated with the rat that Dee (very reluctantly) "rescued" from the laboratory and kept quiet about the subject when their history teacher arrived. As the teacher went on with the lesson, Diana stared dreamily at Dee, implying that she had a crush on him, until she got caught by the teacher and was told to answer a question. Diana, not knowing the answer, looked at Dee for help but he tricked her and got her embarrass herself since he was tired of being stared at by her, which caused the teacher to insult her so Diana got back at Dee for her humiliation by telling the teacher that he was hiding a rat in his bag, resulting in Dee's father being called to the school.

Unfortunately for Diana, her plan did not work since Dee managed to stay out of trouble by recalling Glam's words from earlier that day about being allowed to keep secrets after Glam tried to check Dee's room to find the rat and so Glam remains unaware (albeit suspicious) of that day's events.

## Appearance

Diana is a short, slim, and fair skinned girl with almond shaped grey eyes and slender legs. Diana's hair is pink and chest length and the upper back of her face framed with curtain bangs. She has her eyelids shaded in a dark grey eyeshadow, sports a white turtleneck with black stretch pants and white sneakers.

## Personality

She is mostly nice and likes animals such as rats but despite being nice she is no pushover since she told on Dee for hiding a rat in his bag as revenge for embarrassing her in front of the whole class.
