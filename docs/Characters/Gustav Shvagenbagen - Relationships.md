Mary Shvagenbagen

Mary felt fear and extreme caution in front of Gustav, so she had to keep her mouth shut in order to avoid trouble. However, no matter what, Mary still tried to intercede for her son, noticing her husband crossing all borders but it was to no avail as Gustav was too stubborn and she was too weak.

Lydia Shvagenbagen

Gustav's attitude towards his daughter and eldest child Lydia, is also not fully disclosed. Even if he saw much greater potential in his daughter, Lydia still lost the trust of her father as shown in "Glam" through the bruise on her cheek made by her father due to him guring out that his son was up to something and resorted to beating the answer out of Lydia in order to nd Glam's secret stash of rock music and the scale model of the city.

In his will, Gustav still complimented Lydia and handed most of his property to her: an estate with a total area of 32 hectares, two mansions, four Rolls-Royce, two Mercedes McLaren, a collection of paintings worth $2,000,000, gold jewelry worth $1000,000, as well as the shares in the rm for the production of billiard balls, while also leaving his collection of watches to his nephew Vallen. However Lydia threw a tantrum over not getting his yacht since the keys had apparently gone missing.

Sebastian "Glam" Shvagenbagen

When Sebastian was a teenager, Gustav always beat his son's arm with a ruler until it bled repeatedly when he made the smallest mistake. In the season 1 nale, Gustav discovered son's rock album, his scale model and personal diary, where Sebastian confessed what he really thinks of his father. Gustav promptly threw them in the trash, keeping the diary and saying that they will discuss son's "literary abilities". Then he claims that Sebastian is going to see a therapist to "x him". Causing Sebastian to run away in order to live the life that truly made him happy and to play the music he actually loved, causing Gustav to be very aggravated and shout at him saying that he was not his son anymore and even if he comes back, he will not let him back in, and how Sebastian chose the Rabble over family. After that, Glam never saw his father again but after receiving help to deal with the trauma and eventually starting a family of his own, Glam forgave his father.

When Gustav died, he didn't leave his son any money or valuables, but instead left him the box that contained Sebastian's broken scale model of the city they lived in. However, the reason for him returning the scale model to Glam remains unknown, either to make up for his wrongdoings or to mock Sebastian beyond the grave, although it’s likely the former.
