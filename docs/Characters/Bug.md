Bug is a friend of Victoria and member of a biker gang.

## Biography

Little is known about Bug's past. At one point he joined a biker gang and became attracted to fellow biker Victoria.

When he met Glam, Bug effortlessly grabbed and threw him at some trash cans when he tried to give Victoria her keys back, with both bikers unaware of what Glam was trying to do. Bug and Victoria shared a laugh at Glam's expense before Bug swept her on his arm and tried to kiss her but she refused by grabbing his face roughly and this angered him enough to slap her so she knocked him out in return but they still remained on good terms and still work together in their garage and arrange competitions to get some extra money.

## Appearance

Bug is an overweight, tall, muscular man with blonde hair and a large mustache braided like that of a viking. He wears a grey cap, a dark red shirt, a grey vest, black biker gloves, dark blue jeans and black boots. He is very strong and can easily pick up Vicky, who weighs a little over 70 kg, with little effort.

## Personality

Bug is a grumpy and violent man who is usually seen frowning and yelling. He doesn't take rejection well and is not above hitting women since he slapped Victoria on the face for not letting him kiss her and sometimes pushes her aside roughly, albeit she doesn't mind that one.
