## Childhood

In "Ideal Tone" and "Glam", as well as the subsequently ocially released comic "Goodbye," Ches' teenage life is shown through interactions with Glam. His life is shown as one of a being polar opposite to Glam's socioeconomically since Ches lived in the industrial zone of town with his alcoholic, verbally abusive mother in a broken down, dusty trailer. As seen in episode 10, Ches and his mother lack a proper relationship, with her being addicted to alcohol, treating him badly, disregarding his interest in music and even going to the point of throwing glass bottles at him. Even with that, Ches still defended his mother from Glam's bad comments towards her and insisted on how she had suered in her life, making her the way she was, and how he had no right to judge her as he doesn't know what she has been through, even threatening to hurt Glam if he spoke badly of her again.

Details in his appearance reveal patched holes, worn-down sneakers, and even cracked ngernails, indicating that he is not in a position to maintain his outward appearance, possibly

even neglecting personal hygiene, per Glam's comment that he "stinks."

Ches' enormous talent goes back to the insane amount of practice, interest and determination he puts in his music despite his lacking resources, evidenced by Ches coming in rst place at an audition at an exceptionally prestigious Conservatory, when teenage Glam (back then known as Sebastian) who practiced hours upon hours and was far more musically gifted, came in second place.

## Band Life

Ches introduced Glam to his at the time unnamed band in S1E10. The band later on took on the title "Who Are Those Freaks On Stage" or "Freaks" for short after that was yelled at them during a performance. Ches seemed to get along well with the other band members, Lordy, Bob, and Glam, being shown as playfully rude with them. In the episode "ThoseMonstersOnStage", S2E3, we are shown that Ches has developed a severe drug addiction. It is unclear if this is a new development or has simply gotten worse since they began touring. The band has become very successful, leading to the oer to preform with Twisted Sister. Their manager shows concern about Ches's drug problem in relation to the band and Glam is sent to ask him to quit. Glam arrives at their shared hotel room and discovers Ches is sending his mother large amounts of loose money in the mail. In exchange for helping him address the envelope, Ches promises Glam he will stop but relapses the same day, putting their relationship on thin ice when Ches arrives to band practice under the inuence. The two have a discussion afterwards where in which Ches, seemingly unbeknownst to himself, expresses all the same concerns Glam has had about his addiction and the broken promise. Ches ushes the remaining drugs and, as a show of trust

and connection, Ches purchases a piercing kit and helps Glam pierce his ears. Fellow band mates hear the piercing and become convinced that the two are having sex, arming Lordy's belief that Glam is a homosexual.

During the next band practice, Ches arrives sober but has only begun playing when he receive a call from someone who knows his still undisclosed legal name and believes it to be his mother. It turns out to be a call from his Aunt Rita to inform him his mother has died. It is implied she died of an overdose using drugs she purchased with Ches's money. This sends Ches into a depressive episode where he drinks excessively and never returns to practice. Eventually, Lordy and Bob give up, asserting that he's likely relapsed again and believe the call was from his dealer. Glam goes after him and nds Ches passed out in an alley, visibly disheveled and in his

green jacket that is now ill tting and torn around the sleeves. He assumes that Ches has relapsed and reacts outwardly, unusual for his character, Ches informs him he hasn't broken his promise to quit drugs and is instead drunk. Glam begins to yell at him until Ches tells him his mother is dead.

He remarks he "thought I could meet her again one day" before breaking down sobbing. Glam helps him up and tells him they have to tell the others. Ches protests but is unable to explain why with his slurred speech, only upsetting Glam further. Glam returns Ches to his bed and is forced to confront the other band members himself, telling them there is a reason for Ches's behaviour but that he can't disclose it himself. The manager comments that he is not surprised and has already found a replacement for Ches, introducing a new character named "Leo" who is the new lead vocalist for their band. Glam tells him that he won't leave Ches and the manager admits he already predicted that and has a disturbingly identical replacement for Glam as well. The remaining members of the band depart, Bob giving Glam both the key's to Ches's car, a panther, noting that they could never get it started without him, along with a closed condom, still under the impression Glam and Ches are in a sexual relationship. Ches does not wake until the next day when he nds out the band has left without them but proposes they work as a duo, calling up a manager who approached him after a "Freaks" show.

Season 2 episode 5 takes place part way through the initial music video, after Glam has seen Victoria but before he has met her and returned her keys. Glam missed the aforementioned audition and plans to miss the next one in order to search for Victoria. He and Chive argue over it, leading to Chive calling Victoria a "cunt with tits", which Glam tells him to stop but he continues to until Glam physically strikes him. Chive elects to leave without him and go to the interview where the manager is disappointment to see he is alone, stating that he doesn't put his faith in "junkies" but gives Chive an advanced payment so he can "reform his image". We see a show with his new band, "The Green Sperm Whales", who is visually very similar to his previous band. During an after party for the concert, Chive is shown to have relapsed and has a line of cocaine on the bar in front of him. After a brief conversation with a groupie where in which he laments his troubles with Glam, he over doses on cocaine. He is seen meeting his recently deceased mother in the afterlife and, after apologising for never returning to visit, he states he's a "bad son" because he can't stay with her and wakes up in the hospital, the doctors actively

declaring him dead when he does and whispers "come back".

Present Life

He is shown to own a store where he apparently sells guitars and other music related stu. Unlike Glam, Ches appears to still be single and childless. He and Glam are shown to still be friends and Ches became a sort of surrogate uncle to Glam's sons, even passing down the green jacket he wore in his teen years to Heavy and in the pilot Ches is shown carrying a younger Dee on his shoulders in one of Glam and Vicky's family pictures.

Ches, despite now being an adult, seems to be irresponsible enough to the point where he arrives one and a half hours late to Glam's house after he is invited to play cards with his family. He also has the habit of entering Glam's house through the window.

He has been repeatedly named as an honorary uncle to both Dee and Heavy and is considered a sort of relative to them. So it's safe to say he is a member of the family.
