### Relationships

- **Likes:** Alcohol; Smoking; Watching TV

Ches' mother is the late mother of Ches.

## Appearance

Ches' mother was a plump woman with wavy brown hair who wore a short sleeved rusty red or lilac short sleeved dress. Little is known about her full appearance since only a part of torso was seen.

## Personality

She was very hostile towards her son, insulting him and his music and even throwing bottles of beer in his direction, but according to Ches it was because she faced a lot of terrible things in her life.

However, in S2 E5, she was strangely accepting of Ches during his near death experience. This could've been since she is dead and at peace.

She often drinks because she is unhappy. When drunk, she says inappropriate things.

After work, she preferred to relax with a bottle and TV.

## Trivia

- According to Glam, her name may start with either "Har" or "Var" albeit it is unknown if this is true as Glam had difficulty reading the envelope of the letter Ches was going to send her.
- Glam also read the words "Har" or "Var" in Ches' name, which would mean that Ches and his mother have similar names.
