Anna is a quick-tempered, a little reckless and rather aggressive person. Sharp and rude, does not like being treated disrespectfully and reacts very negatively to it. According to Victoria, it can be judged that she shares feminist views. She also does not like a large number of men.

Anna also helped Vicky win the arm wrestling match by fueling her anger by calling Glam "blondie" (a very rude nickname in Russia). She was also the one filming Vicky's Q&A and reading the questions to her.

**appearance**
Anna has a tall and thin physique. Anna is usually seen wearing her biker suit: a very light blue, almost white colored, full body biker sport-suit, with light blue colored details of the collar, shoulders and undersides of the suit.

Her blue hair tends to the side in a thin, single braid, a side shave with two smaller head-tight braids and side bangs. She wears blue eyeshadow on her eyelids and darker colored accents around her eyes. She also has an eyebrow piercing.
