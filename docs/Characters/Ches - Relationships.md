# Ches/Relationships

Source: https://metalfamily.fandom.com/wiki/Ches/Relationships

These are the relationships of Ches

## Family

Unnamed mother

Ches past is not known for sure before he met Glam, which means that their relationship with his mother is unknown. Ches mother was against her son's activities and his craving for music, wanting him to quickly get a job at the factory.

Both of them can openly insult each other and not even feel guilty for it, but at the same time, Ches does not allow someone else to speak badly about her. Ches is not strongly attached to his mother, but sympathizes with her, apparently due to the fact that in the past she suered immensely. When Ches found out about the death of his mother, he became hysterical, despite her attitude towards him. In S2, E5, he overdoses at one of his new bands after parties, sending him into a near death experience where he comes to terms with her death somewhat, before

coming back to life on the operating table.

## Friends

Glam

Glam is Ches' childhood friend. The two met during adolescence and bonded over their mutual love for music, especially rock music. The two boys continued to be friends well into their adult years and Ches became somewhat of an honorary family member. Ches and Glam are very close and friendly, considering themselves best friends. They both say they know each other better than anyone else. So much so, Glam predicted his excuse in a series about poker, where he knew for sure that Ches would start talking about something like "walking grandmothers across the road." Ches is also a good friend of the Metal family.

Some fans have also pointed Ches is the one who inspired Glam to run away from his abusive household and stand up to his father. Without Ches introducing him to metal and befriending him, it's unknown how Glam would've ended up - or if he'd still be alive. When they were in their rst band, the others expected Glam to confront Ches on his drug use, as they were the closest to one another.

Victoria

Although they haven't interacted a lot on screen, Vicky and Ches are okay with each other, even if she thinks he is "kind of strange".

Heavy

They haven't spoken much to each other, but Chive cares about him. They are also shown multiple times interacting positively with each other. Heavy is also seen multiple times with a jacket similar to Chive's, possibly being Chive's actual old jacket.

Dee

Chive has said that Dee is his favorite. In the animated music video, a picture of young Dee on Chive's shoulders is placed in the photobook. He is shown looking up at Dee while he is learning how to do the 'rock on' hand gesture.
