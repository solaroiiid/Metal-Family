Chesnok, also simply as Ches, (Chive in English dub) — is Glam's bandmate and best friend. He and Glam met in their late teen hood, where Ches helped Glam find true happiness in metal music.

Just as Ches was the one to give Glam his nickname, Chesnok is the nickname given by Glam to him originally intended as an insult due to him being "stinky and green like young sprout of garlic." This was changed in the English dub to accommodate to language barriers, so he's known and referred to as "Chive" in the English translation. His true name is still unknown.

## Appearance

During Ches' young adulthood, he had a lean physique that turned slightly more full as he grew up. He kept his tan skin, brown fluffy hair and dark grey eyes. Ches also has lost one of his front teeth. He is usually seen wearing a maroon coloured shirt with a dark green jacket over it, jeans, and a necklace which had yellow circle charms. In his "band days", he has scruffy facial hair both in the form of a wispy mustache a few hairs on his chin. He is seen wearing a black trench coat and full black outfit when performing. In adulthood, his hair has been grown out and he has a light beard. He is commonly seen with a gray shirt, a plaid jacket, and jeans. He wears a few pieces of jewellery such as a necklace, wrist cuffs, bracelet, and ear lobe piercings.
