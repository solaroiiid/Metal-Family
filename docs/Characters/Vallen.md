## Gallery

Vallen is the nephew of Gustav Shvagenbagen and the paternal cousin of Lydia and Glam.

## Biography

Vallen was present with his cousins during the reading of Gustav's will and smiled from ear to ear upon finding out he had inherited his uncle's collection of golden watches. Like Lydia, Vallen seemed to only care about his inheritance and paid no heed to his long lost cousin or his wife.

## Appearance

Vallen has short blonde hair and an undercut and fair skin, his face and nose are similar to Gustav's.
